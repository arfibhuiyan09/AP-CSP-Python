Unit 4.1 The Internet

computing device: a physical device that can run a program
    Examples: Computers, Tablets, Routers, etc

computing system: a *group* of computing devices working together to achieve a goal
    Examples: Laptops, Computers, Tablets

computer network: a group of interconnected devices sharing/recieving data
    - is a type of computing system

routing: the process of finding a path from the sender to reciever
    - routing on the internet is usually dynamic

bandwidth: the max data a network could hold at a certain time
    - measured in bps

* the internet was designed to be scalable
    - Scalability: the ability to adjust capacity of something
* info is passed as data streams in packets
    - packets contain a small amount of data used to route the data to the sender
* IP, TCP, UDP, and HTTP/HTTPS are all internet protocols
* WWW uses the internet
